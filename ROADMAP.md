# Manyhands Roadmap

A minimalist god-game that respects Populous' core loop: sculpt terrain, watch followers flourish, then nurture growth. The world is always on when someone is playing.

## Current (v0.1.0)
✅ Local isometric island with Raise, Lower, Forest, Settle, Shrine, Bless  
✅ House progression: hut → cottage → manor → citadel  
✅ Follower settlement and migration on leveled land  
✅ Faith economy and convergence goals  
✅ Shared worlds via Supabase + real-time voice (foyer)  
✅ Populous-inspired title phases (emerging → ascendant)  
✅ PWA install + offline local play  

## Phase 2: Follower Agency (Civilization flavor)
- [ ] **Named followers** visible on the map with simple personalities (personality traits affect settlement preferences)
- [ ] **Wandering traders** move between settlements, exchange goods
- [ ] **Disaster events** (meteor, plague, drought) that threaten population — players must respond
- [ ] **Rally command** (influence a group to move/gather) — costs faith, visibly rewarding

## Phase 3: Terrain Dynamics
- [ ] **Rivers & irrigation** — water flows downhill, supports denser settlements
- [ ] **Biome painting** — forest, savanna, tundra with different fertility/costs
- [ ] **Volcanos** — sculptable peaks that occasionally erupt (high-stakes terrain feature)
- [ ] **Weather effects** — rain refills faith faster, storms damage fragile buildings

## Phase 4: Social & Conflict (Warcraft RTS elements)
- [ ] **Rival tribes** on the same shared island (cosmetic, non-combat but competitive)
- [ ] **Monument building** — collective goal that requires multiple players' faith contribution
- [ ] **Blessing vs. curse** duality — benevolent and destructive powers coexist
- [ ] **Legacy buildings** — shrines and citadels persist and grant bonuses to new followers

## Phase 5: Cosmology (Endless progression)
- [ ] **Ascension goal** — reach 500+ followers and unlock sky islands
- [ ] **Mythology** — unlock god-form avatar after completing regional goals
- [ ] **Cross-island gateway** — established islands can connect and merge
- [ ] **Archive** — completed worlds save permanently, visible as monuments in new worlds

## Design Principles
- **Terrain is the toy.** Every action sculpts; followers respond to beauty and practicality.
- **Faith is the pacing mechanism.** Limits power use; grows from social thriving, not grinding.
- **Followers are the story.** Visible, named, with gentle preferences — not statistics.
- **No combat.** Conflict is territorial and aesthetic; players shape different visions.
- **Always on when needed.** World steps while any player is present; pauses when empty.
- **Voice as belonging.** Optional, island-wide, real-time peer mesh (no Discord needed).

---

Inspired by Populous (SNES), Civilization series (sandbox mastery), and Netquake (collaborative play in a shared world).
